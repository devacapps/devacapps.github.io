DreamSeason Terms of Use

Version 3 · Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: DreamSeason connects families with people who want to help pay for youth activities. It never handles money, takes no fee, never stores who your child is, and every gift happens on the provider's own site.

1. What DreamSeason Is

DreamSeason is a directory where parents list youth activity costs and benefactors choose ones to fund. It is not a charity, crowdfunding platform, bank, payment processor, or party to any gift.

2. Who Can Use It

You must be 18 or older and live in the United States. Only a parent or legal guardian may post a request, and only for their own child's activity costs. DreamSeason uses your iCloud account to store and show requests; it does not create a separate account.

3. No Money Passes Through DreamSeason

Gifts are purchases made in Safari from the family's Amazon, Target, or Walmart wishlist or registry, under those stores' terms and privacy policies. DreamSeason takes no fee, and no money ever passes to DreamSeason or directly to the family. DreamSeason cannot see payments or issue refunds.

4. No Guarantees

DreamSeason does not verify requests, invoices, or how gifts are used. Posting a request does not guarantee it will be funded. A gift is voluntary, is not a loan or a purchase from DreamSeason, and is not a tax-deductible charitable donation.

5. Parent Responsibilities

Post only real, current costs at the amount on the invoice. Link only to an Amazon, Target, or Walmart wishlist or registry you control. Add the items or gift cards you need to that list rather than sharing an email address. Use what you receive for the activity described, and mark the request Funded once it is covered. You may send one thank-you note per request; it must follow the same privacy rules as a request. You can have up to 5 active requests, and each one leaves Discover after 60 days unless you renew it.

6. Protecting Your Child's Privacy

Never include a child's name, photo, school, team, address, or contact details in a title or summary. Invoice text is read and redacted on your device, but redaction can miss details, so review the summary before posting. The invoice photo is never uploaded or saved. If you add a state, only the state is shown.

7. Benefactor Responsibilities

Check the destination site before you pay. Give only through the links on a request. Do not try to identify, find, or contact any family or child.

8. Zero Tolerance for Objectionable Content

DreamSeason has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Requests that identify a child, are fraudulent or misleading, harass anyone, contain offensive or sexual content, ask for payment outside the posted links, or link to unsafe or unrelated sites are prohibited.

9. Reporting and Moderation

Anyone can report a request from its detail screen, and you can hide every request from a poster. Moderators aim to review reports within 24 hours, or within 48 hours when report volume is high; review times are goals, not guarantees. DreamSeason may remove any request and turn off posting for anyone who breaks these terms, without notice. Contact devacapps.app@gmail.com with questions.

10. Your Data

Requests and thank-you notes you post are stored in DreamSeason's iCloud database and can be seen by anyone signed in to the app, along with an anonymous identifier Apple assigns to your account. Your name, email, and Apple Account details are never shared. Your role, terms acceptance, and hidden posters stay on this device. Delete All My Requests in Account removes everything you posted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

11. Notifications

Alerts are optional and off until you turn them on in Account. They are used only for new requests and moderation notices, never for advertising.

12. Third-Party Sites

Funding links open in Safari. DreamSeason does not control or endorse those sites and is not responsible for their content, availability, or security.

13. Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability

DreamSeason is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. To the extent allowed by law, DreamSeason is not liable for losses arising from requests, gifts, or third-party sites.

14. Changes to These Terms

When these terms change, the app shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you continue.
