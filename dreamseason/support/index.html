Response times

We aim to review reported requests within 24 hours. When report volume is high, a review can take up to 48 hours. We answer support email as soon as we can, usually within a few business days.

A child's safety at risk?

DreamSeason can't respond to emergencies. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. To report the exploitation of a child, use the NCMEC CyberTipline. Then report the request in the app so it can be removed.

Safety & Your Data
What does "3 funded · 2 thank-yous" mean?

It's the family's history on DreamSeason, shown on each request. These are counts, not a rating. Posted is every request the family has shared. Funded is how many they marked as funded; DreamSeason can't see payments, so the family reports this themselves. Thank-yous are notes they sent benefactors, and only notes from the family's own account count. Requests a moderator removed aren't counted. Benefactors never rate families, and no names or identities are shown. A new family simply has no history yet.

Report a request

Open the request, tap the … menu, and choose Report Request. Reports are anonymous. A moderator reviews each one and removes requests that break the Terms of Use.

Stop seeing someone's requests

Open one of their requests, tap the … menu, and choose Hide Requests from This Poster. Only you stop seeing them, and they aren't told. Undo it in Account > Your Data.

Delete my data

Account > Your Data > Delete All My Requests removes everything you posted, for everyone. Deleting the app removes what's stored on your device. You can also email us to ask for removal. See the Privacy Policy.

Keep a child anonymous

Never include a name, photo, school, team, or address in a title or summary. DreamSeason blocks emails, phone numbers, addresses, and links in text, but always read the summary before posting.

Giving & Payments
Does DreamSeason take payments?

No. Every gift is a purchase from the family's Amazon, Target, or Walmart wishlist, made in Safari on the store's own site. DreamSeason never sees card details, holds money, or takes a fee, so it can't issue refunds. For help with a payment, contact the provider you paid.

Which links can parents add?

The share link of an Amazon, Target, or Walmart wishlist or registry. For fees like league registration, parents add gift cards they can use to pay them. To accept gift cards, add them to that list: the retailer ships them without showing buyers your email or address. Link shorteners aren't allowed.

Are requests verified?

No. DreamSeason doesn't verify requests, invoices, or how gifts are used, and gifts aren't tax-deductible donations. Give only what you're comfortable giving, and only through the links on a request.

For Parents
Send a thank-you note

Once gifts come in, swipe the request right in My Requests and tap Say Thank You. You can send one short note per request, and it can't be edited later. Benefactors who marked I Gave are notified; they can't reply. Keep it anonymous: no names, photos, schools, or teams.

My request was removed

A moderator hid it because it broke the Terms of Use, for example by including a child's details or an unsafe link. The reason is shown in My Requests. Delete it and post a corrected one, or email us with your Support ID if you think it was a mistake.

Expiring and renewing

Requests leave Discover after 60 days so the list stays current. Expired requests stay in My Requests: swipe right and tap Renew to show one for another 60 days.

Track gifts and mark a request funded

DreamSeason can't see payments, so you keep the total up to date. Swipe a request right and tap Received to enter what has come in. Reaching the goal marks it Funded and removes it from Discover. Use Reopen if it still needs help.

Troubleshooting
"Sign In to iCloud"

Requests are stored in DreamSeason's iCloud database, so an Apple Account is needed. Your name and email are never shared. Open Settings, sign in to your Apple Account, then return to DreamSeason and tap Try Again.

"Couldn't load requests" or Discover is empty

Check your connection and tap Try Again. If filters are set, tap Show Everything. If it keeps happening, email us.

A funding link is marked in red

Copy the list's full share address, including https://. Wishlists and registries must be from Amazon, Target, or Walmart, and link shorteners aren't allowed.

The invoice scan found nothing

Use a clear, well-lit photo of the printed invoice, flat and straight on. You can also skip the scan and type the summary yourself. The photo is read on your device and never uploaded.
