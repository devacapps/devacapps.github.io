DreamSeason

For iPhone and iPad

Help a kid get a season of soccer, piano, robotics, dance, or camp. Families post the real cost of an activity. You cover it directly on a trusted site like Stripe, Amazon, or Target.

How it works

  • A parent posts a cost. League fees, an instrument, a camp. The invoice is read on their phone, and personal details are removed before anything is shared.
  • You find one that matters to you. Browse requests by activity and state. Each one shows the exact amount needed.
  • You give on a trusted site. Pay through the parent's Stripe Payment Link, or buy an item or gift card from their Amazon, Target, or Walmart list.

Built to keep kids private

  • No child names, photos, schools, or addresses.
  • DreamSeason never touches money and charges no fees.
  • Anyone can report a request, and moderators aim to review reports within 24 hours.
