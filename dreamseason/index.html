DreamSeason

For iPhone and iPad

Help a kid get a season of soccer, piano, robotics, dance, or camp. Families post the real cost of an activity. You cover it directly by buying from the family's Amazon, Target, or Walmart wishlist.

Why DreamSeason exists

Talent and curiosity show up in every neighborhood. Money doesn't. When registration fees, gear, and lessons don't fit the budget, activities are often the first thing a family has to give up, and a kid ends up watching from the sidelines.

You don't have to be wealthy to change that. A $10 gift toward cleats or a month of lessons can be the difference between a kid who plays and a kid who waits. DreamSeason connects families facing those costs with people who have a little to spare, directly and privately.

How it works

  • A parent posts a cost. League fees, an instrument, a camp. The invoice is read on their phone, and personal details are removed before anything is shared.
  • You find one that matters to you. Browse requests by activity and state. Each one shows the exact amount still needed.
  • You choose your gift. Slide to any amount from $5 to the full cost, then buy items or a gift card adding up to about that much from the family's Amazon, Target, or Walmart list. The store ships it to them without showing you their address.

Built to keep kids private

  • No child names, photos, schools, or addresses.
  • DreamSeason never touches money and charges no fees.
  • Anyone can report a request, and moderators aim to review reports within 24 hours.
