DreamSeason Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

DreamSeason collects as little as possible: the requests parents choose to post, and the reports people send. No names, emails, phone numbers, locations, contacts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

1. What We Collect

Requests a parent posts: title, category, amount, amount received so far, optional state, invoice summary, and funding links. Reports: the reason and any note the reporter adds. An anonymous identifier Apple assigns to each iCloud account in DreamSeason, used to know who can edit a request. Alert settings, if you turn alerts on.

2. What We Don't Collect

We never collect or receive your name, email, phone number, Apple Account details, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information, or browsing history. DreamSeason has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

3. Invoice Photos

When you scan an invoice, the photo is read on your device to fill in the summary and amount. The photo is never uploaded or saved.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Requests, reports, and moderation decisions are stored in DreamSeason's database on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Your role, terms acceptance, and hidden posters are stored only on your device.

5. Who Can See It

Requests can be seen by anyone signed in to DreamSeason. Reports can be seen only by DreamSeason's moderators. Gifts happen on third-party sites, which have their own privacy policies.

6. How Long We Keep It

Requests stay until the parent or a moderator deletes them. Expired and funded requests are hidden from Discover but kept so the parent can reopen or renew them. Reports are deleted when their request is deleted.

7. Your Choices

Delete All My Requests in Account removes everything you posted. You can turn alerts off at any time. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

8. Children

DreamSeason is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. Requests must not contain any information about a child's identity.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: devacapps.app@gmail.com.
